# Orbit Bank brand guidelines

## Who we are

Orbit Bank is a fictional community bank built for people who want clear, dependable ways to manage everyday money. Our service combines practical digital tools with knowledgeable local teams. We help customers understand their options, plan with confidence, and make informed decisions at their own pace. We are a financial partner, not a shortcut to wealth, and our writing must never promise a particular outcome.

The bank was founded in 2012. Orbit Bank operates 42 branches across its fictional service area. Orbit Saver currently offers a variable 4.25% annual percentage yield (APY), effective August 1, 2026. Rates can change. The APY is for illustration in this sample guideline and should always be checked against the current approved rate sheet before it appears in customer communications. Do not calculate or invent an account balance, earnings estimate, eligibility result, or future rate from this figure.

## Our voice

Write with calm confidence. Use everyday words, short sentences, and a helpful tone. Explain one idea at a time. Be warm without sounding overly familiar, and be confident without sounding certain about a customer's financial future. Address readers directly as “you” when it helps make an instruction clear. Use “we” when describing what Orbit Bank does.

Respect the reader's ability to make a decision. Offer a clear next step, such as reviewing account details or speaking with a banker. Avoid pressure, countdowns, shame, fear, or language that suggests a person has failed if they do not act immediately. Do not imply that a product is right for every person. When a choice depends on an individual's circumstances, say so and invite them to compare the relevant terms.

## Words and claims to avoid

Never call an Orbit Bank account or product “risk-free.” Do not say an account will “guarantee returns,” “guarantee savings,” or guarantee any financial result. Do not promise “instant approval”; applications are subject to review and eligibility requirements. Avoid “best bank,” “number one,” “unbeatable,” and “perfect for everyone” unless a specific claim has current, approved evidence and legal review. This sample guideline does not provide that evidence.

Do not use “free” to describe a service unless the current fee schedule confirms that no fee applies to the described service and relevant conditions are shown. Do not describe a variable rate as fixed, permanent, or locked in. Never turn an APY into a guaranteed amount of interest. Do not compare Orbit Bank's rates, fees, or services with a competitor unless an approved comparison source is provided for the same time period and product terms.

If a sentence includes an outcome claim, make the limits clear. “You could earn interest” is different from “you will earn a certain amount.” Do not use customer testimonials, survey results, rankings, or social proof unless the source and approval are available. Do not invent customer counts, locations, awards, or time savings.

## Required disclosures

When an advertisement discusses a deposit account's yield or a rate that may change, place the current rate and its conditions close to the claim. Label the figure as APY, state that it is variable, and include the date the rate was checked. Do not rely on a footnote that readers may miss. If eligibility, minimum balance, or other account conditions apply, include them beside the offer or direct readers to a clearly named terms page.

Use this exact disclaimer whenever the content discusses an investment product: “Investment products are not bank deposits, are not insured by any government agency, and may lose value.” Keep the full sentence together. Do not shorten it to “investments may lose value,” and do not imply that a bank deposit and an investment product have the same protections. If the content does not discuss an investment product, do not add this disclaimer as generic decoration.

Never describe a savings account as an investment product. Deposit accounts have their own account terms and applicable protections. If a draft is unclear about whether it is describing a deposit account, an investment, or another service, stop and ask Compliance to review it before publication.

## Formatting and accessibility

Use “Orbit Bank” with both words capitalized. Use “Orbit Saver” for the product name. Write “annual percentage yield (APY)” the first time it appears, then use “APY.” Keep percentages attached to their number, as in “4.25% APY.” Use dates with the month written out in customer-facing copy to reduce ambiguity. Do not use all caps for emphasis, and do not rely on color alone to communicate a warning or decision.

Use descriptive link text that tells readers what will happen, such as “Review Orbit Saver terms.” Avoid vague links like “click here.” Keep headings in a logical order, provide meaningful alternative text for informative images, and make sure instructions work for keyboard users and screen readers. If a graphic contains a rate, repeat the rate and its conditions in nearby text.

## Review before publishing

Confirm that the product name, rate, date, conditions, and disclaimer match current approved materials. Check that every numerical claim appears in a current source. Remove claims that cannot be supported. Make sure the text does not imply guaranteed results, automatic eligibility, or universal suitability. Ask Compliance when a claim's scope or product type is uncertain.

These guidelines describe Orbit Bank's fictional voice and sample facts for demonstration. They are not real financial advice, current rate information, or legal guidance.
